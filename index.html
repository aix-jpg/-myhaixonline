About Monster Survivors

Monster Survivors is an action-packed survival game where you face waves of increasingly challenging monsters. Your goal is simple: survive as long as possible while unlocking powerful upgrades and abilities.

Inspired by the best rogue-like games, Monster Survivors offers a unique experience with each playthrough. No two games are ever the same, as you'll encounter different enemy combinations and power-up opportunities.

As you progress, you'll collect experience points to level up your character, unlocking new abilities and enhancing existing ones to combat the ever-growing monster threat.

Survival Tips

  1. Keep moving: Standing still is a quick way to get surrounded by monsters. Stay mobile to avoid being cornered.
  2. Prioritize upgrades: Choose upgrades that complement your playstyle and work well together for maximum effectiveness.
  3. Collect experience gems: Don't miss out on experience points dropped by defeated monsters - they're essential for leveling up.
  4. Know your enemies: Learn the attack patterns of different monster types to better anticipate and avoid their attacks.
  5. Manage space: Use the entire arena to your advantage, creating distance between yourself and large groups of enemies.
